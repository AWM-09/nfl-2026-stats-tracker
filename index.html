Tracker notes

DATA STATUS
The dashboard tries to load the latest weekly player statistics from nflverse. Statistics are restricted to the 2026 regular season and the available Weeks 1–6. Some advanced/defensive fields may be blank if the feed does not provide them.
Completion percentage is calculated from total completions ÷ total attempts for cumulative views, not by averaging weekly percentages. This avoids misleading totals.
Data source: nflverse-data releases (public player-stat datasets; attribution required under the applicable data licence). This dashboard is an independent fan-made tool, not affiliated with the NFL. Auto-refresh depends on the source being reachable and current. If your browser blocks the data request, download a player-stat CSV from nflverse and use “Upload CSV”.
